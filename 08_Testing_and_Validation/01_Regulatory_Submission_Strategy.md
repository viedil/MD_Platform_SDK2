# Regulatory Submission Strategy

## Deliverables

1. **SOUP Boundary Documentation**: FMEDA, safety manual, test reports
2. **IEC 62304 Traceability**: Auto-generated, auditable
3. **Cybersecurity Package**: SBOM, CVE response, pentest report
4. **Clinical Validation**: PCCP template, shadow logs, physician feedback
5. **Submission Ready**: FDA 510(k)/De Novo, EU MDR Technical File, PMDA guidance

## Process

* Engage notified body early
* Use pre-validated templates
* Automate documentation generation
* Maintain change control for updates
